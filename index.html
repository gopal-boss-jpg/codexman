How long does a website take?
Most sites launch in three to five weeks. Stores and web apps take longer, and we give you a dated schedule before we start.

      
Will I own the website?
Yes. On the final payment you get the code, the design files and the hosting and domain accounts, all in your name.

      
Can I edit the content myself?
Yes. We set up an editor for text and images, then run a short training session so your team can update pages on their own.

      
Do you help after launch?
We offer monthly care plans covering hosting, backups, security updates and small changes. You can also come back for new features whenever you need them.

      
How do payments work?
40% to start, 30% when the design is approved and 30% at launch. Everything is written into the quote.
